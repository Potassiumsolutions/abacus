Learn the Soroban

Each rod (column) is one digit, 0–9. Beads only count when pushed toward the middle bar.

Heaven bead above the bar = 5 when slid down.

Earth beads below (four) = 1 each when slid up.

To add, push more beads toward the bar; to subtract, pull them away. Fill past 9? Carry to the rod on its left.

Later cities use simpler ten-bead rods — just slide up to nine beads, each worth 1.
